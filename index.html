01 About

I'm a software developer with 6+ years of experience building payment, media, and real-time systems in Go; from fintech and events platforms to cybersecurity tooling and products for growing teams. Somewhere along the way I fell in love with Bitcoin, and it stuck.

Since 2024 I've been a Btrust Builder, contributing to open-source Bitcoin projects like Zeus LN, btcd, and Fedimint's clientd, and building a Bitcoin network explorer from scratch. I write about protocol internals (hash functions, HD wallets, mining) on Medium, partly to teach, mostly to make sure I actually understand what I'm shipping.

I'm building Ipilẹ Labs, a Bitcoin & Lightning startup aimed at both everyday users and businesses across Africa, while continuing to learn and grow as a Bitcoin protocol contributor.

As a Btrust Builder, I've also spent about two years teaching cohorts through Mastering Bitcoin, Rust for Bitcoiners, and Learning Bitcoin from the Command Line; helping newer builders find their footing in the ecosystem.
