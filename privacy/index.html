Visitor analytics

This portfolio supports Umami analytics to understand visits over time, approximate countries, device categories, referral sources, and which pages are useful. Analytics are only enabled after a website ID and tracker are configured. No visitor statistics are displayed publicly.

When enabled, events record actions such as opening projects, viewing or downloading a résumé, using a demo, following a professional link, or successfully sending a contact form. Contact names, email addresses, message text, and form contents are never attached to analytics events.

Analytics respect the browser’s Do Not Track preference. You can also disable collection for this browser using the control below. Your preference and selected color theme are stored locally.

Contact messages

The contact form sends your name, email, and message to Formspree so I can respond. Please only include information needed for the conversation. You can also contact me directly by email or through LinkedIn.

Formspree privacy policy

External services

Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts. Project, GitHub, LinkedIn, and article links open external sites with their own privacy practices. The interactive demonstrations run locally in your browser.
