I’m Omoyemi John Arigbanla, a software engineer. I’ve been programming since 2018, working across product interfaces, backend services, and Bitcoin infrastructure.

I like taking a problem from the first product question through to the code that makes it work. Payment state, subscription rules, and real-time updates are some of the details I work on.

Through Btrust Builders, I mentor engineers working through Bitcoin fundamentals and Rust. Teaching is another way of finding out whether I really understand something.
